## 1 Problem and users

The group described a need for quick information and help with routine tasks. Users include people asking general questions, learners and office workers.

## 2 Most visible layer

Application. Users see a chat screen and read replies. The internal model and supporting services are not visible from that screen.

## 3 Quality dimensions

| Dimension | Record in the group worksheet |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Proposed checking course prerequisites against an official catalogue. No catalogue comparison was recorded. |
| Response time | Replies were reported as taking about 1-2 seconds. No timing log was supplied. |
| Fairness | The worksheet described the system as highly capable but not always correct. It did not record a comparison between user groups. |

## 4 What it did well

The group reported that it explained difficult topics in simple language and broke explanations into steps. For the prompt 'Explain about Malaysia briefly', the reply was described as simple and easy to understand.

## 5 What it did poorly

For the prompt 'Tell me what happened today in Malaysia', the group reported that the tested interaction did not provide the latest news. This is a record of that interaction, not a claim about every version or tool setting.

## 6 Responsible AI risks

- Bias: imbalanced training information could lead to unfair answers and harm affected users.
- Privacy: users may share personal details when asking for personalised help, exposing information they did not need to disclose.

## 7 One improvement

Show sources when an answer depends on outside information. This would help users check the reply.
