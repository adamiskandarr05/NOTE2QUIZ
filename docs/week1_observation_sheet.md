## 1 What problem does this system solve and who benefits

**Problem:** Users need accurate information quickly and help with routine tasks.

**Beneficiaries:** People asking general questions, learners and office workers who want help with routine tasks.

## 2 Which layer is most visible to users

**Layer:** Application.

**Explanation:** The user sees a chat interface and receives answers.

## 3 Quality dimensions and measurement

| Dimension | Question | Measurement or recorded note |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Does the system give correct course prerequisites? | Compare the answer with the official course catalogue. |
| Latency | How fast does it respond? | The group reported about 1-2 seconds. |
| Fairness | Does it answer equally well for all faculties? | The worksheet described it as highly capable, but not always 100% accurate. |

## 4 What the system did well

It explained difficult topics in simple language, broke explanations into steps and gave examples. When asked 'Explain about Malaysia briefly', it gave information that was simple and easy to understand.

## 5 What the system did poorly

It can give incorrect or outdated information while sounding confident. When asked 'Tell me what happened today in Malaysia', the group reported that it could not provide the latest news. Important facts should be checked against reliable sources.

## 6 Responsible AI risks

**Risk 1: Bias.** Training data that favours certain views may lead to biased answers. Users may receive unfair or discriminatory responses.

**Risk 2: Privacy.** Users may share personal information when asking for more personalised answers. This can expose information they did not need to share.

## 7 One improvement

Improve transparency by showing sources for answers that use outside information. This would help users check the response and understand where the information came from.
